# UI Benchmark Rubric

Each output receives a 100-point score. The rubric is intentionally skill-agnostic so landing pages, dashboards, product pages, and multi-page sites can be judged by the same standards.

## Categories

- Product fit and task completion: 20 points
- Visual hierarchy and composition: 20 points
- Distinctiveness without gimmickry: 15 points
- Information architecture and workflow clarity: 15 points
- Responsive resilience and text handling: 10 points
- Interaction states and motion appropriateness: 10 points
- Technical execution and maintainability: 10 points

## Penalties

- Subtract up to 15 points for generic AI defaults: card soup, purple gradient SaaS look, fake bento filler, hero dashboard mockups, weak brand/object signal, or interchangeable copy.
- Subtract up to 15 points for layout failures: overlap, unreadable contrast, awkward mobile wrapping, horizontal scroll, broken navigation, or missing required page/task content.
- Subtract up to 10 points for implementation issues: build failures, runtime errors, missing routes, inaccessible controls, or inconsistent shared styles.

## Evidence Standard

Scores should cite screenshots, route behavior, and code/build evidence. A page can be visually exciting and still lose if it hides the user job, breaks on mobile, or violates the requested task.
